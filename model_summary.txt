Limitations to disclose:
  1. Synthetic Sparkov data - real-world metrics will be lower.
  2. Labels are instant here; production fraud labels arrive after
     chargebacks (weeks) - label latency is a known constraint.
  3. Velocity features are cumulative-history proxies; a live system
     needs true rolling windows computed identically at serving time
     (training-serving skew risk).
